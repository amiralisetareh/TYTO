# TYTO Copyright and Rights Policy

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026  
**Service:** [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot)

TYTO respects authors, artists, publishers, translators, studios and other rights holders. A work appearing in a catalogue or archive does not mean TYTO owns it or has permission to distribute it.

## 1. Submissions

Only submit or publish material that you own, are licensed to use, have permission to share, or are otherwise legally allowed to use. Do not assume that a work is free to distribute merely because it is old or available elsewhere online.

## 2. Report a rights concern

A rights holder or authorized representative may contact the Operator through [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot). Include:
1. Your name and a reliable contact method.
2. Identification of the work concerned.
3. The exact TYTO item, message or file, with a link or identifying details where possible.
4. An explanation of your ownership or authority to act.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that the information in your report is accurate.

Do not send unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 3. Review

The Operator may request clarification, restrict access while reviewing a report, remove material or take another appropriate action. A report does not guarantee a particular outcome or response time.

## 4. Disputed restrictions

If you believe material was restricted by mistake, contact the Operator with the item reference, an explanation and evidence of authorization. The Operator may review the information and restore access where appropriate and lawful.

## 5. Repeat violations

The Operator may restrict users who repeatedly submit material they are not authorized to share or who abuse the reporting process.

## 6. Contact

Send reports through [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot) and mark the message **COPYRIGHT REPORT**.

---

This is an operational reporting policy, not legal advice or a formal designation of a statutory copyright agent. The Operator should add a monitored email or web form before large-scale public launch.
