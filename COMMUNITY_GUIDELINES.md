# TYTO Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026

TYTO aims to be a useful place to discover and organize books, comics, manga and stories. Help keep it respectful and reliable.

## Do
- Treat readers, creators and other users respectfully.
- Provide accurate titles, credits, editions and source information.
- Share only material you are authorized to share.
- Respect copyright, privacy and content warnings.
- Report broken, misleading, unsafe or rights-infringing material.

## Do not
- Harass, threaten, discriminate against or impersonate anyone.
- Publish private information without permission.
- Upload malware, scams, spam or deceptive files.
- Submit content that violates applicable law or Telegram rules.
- Claim ownership of another creator's work.
- Attempt to bypass moderation or technical restrictions.

## Moderation

The Operator may review submissions, remove content, limit features or restrict access when reasonably necessary to protect users, creators or the archive. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent restriction.

## Reporting

Contact [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot) with the relevant item or message reference and a concise description. For copyright concerns, follow [COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md).

**Be curious. Credit creators. Preserve stories responsibly.**
