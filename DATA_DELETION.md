# TYTO Data Access, Correction and Deletion Requests

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026  
**Service:** [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot)

This document explains how to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of TYTO.

## 1. Submit a request

Contact the Operator through [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot). Start your message with one of:
- **DATA ACCESS** — ask what personal information may be associated with your use.
- **DATA CORRECTION** — request correction of inaccurate information.
- **DATA DELETION** — request deletion of personal information where technically and legally possible.

Include your Telegram user ID if available, the relevant message or item reference, and enough detail to locate the information. Never send passwords or login codes.

## 2. Verification

The Operator may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request concerns you. Verification should be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

## 3. Scope and limitations

The Operator can only act on information under their control. Information held by Telegram or hosting providers is subject to their own policies and request procedures.

Some records may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, rights enforcement or system integrity. Blocking the bot or deleting a Telegram conversation does not necessarily delete records already stored by the Operator or third parties.

## 4. Processing the request

The Operator should review requests within a reasonable period and communicate the outcome, subject to applicable law and the complexity of the request. The exact deletion process depends on the deployed database, backups and logging configuration.

## 5. Contact

Submit requests via [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot), clearly marking the message with the request type above.

---

This document describes a request process; it does not claim that automated account or data deletion is currently implemented. The Operator must verify the deployed system before launch.
