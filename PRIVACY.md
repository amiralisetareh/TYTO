# TYTO Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026  
**Service:** TYTO Telegram bot — [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot)  
**Operator:** The person or entity operating TYTO ("the Operator").

TYTO is intended to help people discover and organize information about books, comics, manga and stories. This policy explains the privacy approach for the bot.

> **Deployment notice:** The repository currently contains only a basic README. The Operator must verify this policy against the deployed bot, hosting, database, logs and integrations before launch. This document does not assert that any particular data collection or deletion feature has already been implemented.

## 1. Information that may be processed

When you use a Telegram bot, Telegram may make information available to its operator, depending on the interaction. This can include:
- Telegram user ID, username, display name and language setting;
- messages, commands, button selections and content you send;
- file identifiers and related metadata for files you submit.

The deployed service may also generate technical information such as error logs, timestamps and security events. The exact information processed depends on the live implementation and its configuration.

## 2. Why information may be used

Information should only be used as necessary to:
- respond to commands and provide TYTO features;
- search, organize and display catalogue information;
- handle support, privacy and copyright requests;
- protect the service, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors;
- meet applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Telegram and hosting providers

TYTO operates through Telegram. Telegram processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The Operator may also use hosting, database, storage or other infrastructure providers. Those providers may process information as needed to operate the service.

TYTO does not claim that conversations with the bot are end-to-end encrypted between the user and the Operator.

## 4. Storage and retention

The Operator should retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, and then delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible, subject to legal and security needs.

Actual retention periods must be documented by the Operator after confirming the deployed database, backups and log configuration.

## 5. Sharing

The Operator should not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized people who maintain TYTO and to Telegram or infrastructure providers where necessary to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or needed to protect rights and safety.

## 6. Your choices and requests

You can stop interacting with the bot by blocking it. This does not necessarily delete information already held by the Operator or Telegram.

For a privacy request, contact the Operator through [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot). Include your Telegram user ID if available and describe the request. The Operator may need to verify the request before acting. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

## 7. Security

The Operator should apply appropriate access controls, protect bot tokens and credentials, restrict administrative access, and keep software and infrastructure maintained. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children

TYTO is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from children. Users should not send passwords, payment details, government identifiers, precise home addresses or other sensitive information to the bot.

## 9. Changes

This policy may be updated when TYTO's features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.

## 10. Contact

Privacy requests: [@titoarchivebot](https://t.me/titoarchivebot).
